How the read-only check works

  1. Candidates and requirements are read into memory.
  2. The edited requirement set is applied to that copy only.
  3. Both rankings are recomputed with the same scoring rules.
  4. A digest of every domain row is compared before and after.
  5. A difference refuses the result rather than hiding it.
