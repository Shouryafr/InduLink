Privacy

Privacy notice

This is a prototype running on synthetic demo data. It has not been through an institutional privacy review, and it must not hold real student or company data until it has.

Not for real data yet

Every record in this environment is synthetic. Do not enter personal data about a real person, or confidential material from a real organisation.

What is collected

  • Account details: e-mail address, display name, role and a password stored as a bcrypt hash.
  • Profile detail you choose to enter: skills, interests, coursework, experience, availability.
  • Evidence you submit, and the review notes a reviewer writes about it.
  • Challenge and project records, including requirement versions and confirmed assignments.
  • Security events: sign-in attempts, authorization denials, and changes to important records.

Who can see it

  • Your profile is private by default. You choose whether it is visible to your institution or public.
  • Evidence review notes are visible only to you, the reviewer and administrators.
  • Evaluations and feedback are restricted to the authorised roles for that project.
  • Confidential files require an acknowledgement before access, and every access is audited.
  • Administrators can read the audit log. They cannot edit or delete it.

Retention

Session records expire 30 days after sign-in and are revoked on sign-out or a password change. Audit events are retained and append-only. Accounts are anonymised rather than deleted, so history stays consistent. Retention windows are configurable by an administrator.

Your controls

  • Change what your profile exposes, at any time, from your profile page.
  • Ask an administrator to export or delete your data (this build does not yet automate it).
  • Appeal any contribution or passport entry you believe is wrong.

Security choices worth stating plainly

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt at cost 12; plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Authorization is enforced on the server for every protected read and write, not by hiding buttons.
  • Uploaded files are checked by content, stored outside the web root, and never executed.
  • This build makes no claim to be free of security flaws. Residual risks are listed in the repository's RISKS.md.

This notice describes a prototype. It is not legal advice and it is not a substitute for your institution's own privacy notice.
