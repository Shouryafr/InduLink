Terms, ownership and confidentiality

What follows is the platform's own baseline. It is deliberately short, and your institution's terms replace it once they are published.

Terms to be confirmed by your institution

No legal terms have been published for this deployment. Text on this page is a placeholder describing how the prototype behaves, not a contract. An administrator must publish the institution's terms, with a version and date, before this platform is used with real projects.

Ownership of deliverables

Ownership, reuse and disclosure terms for project deliverables are set by the agreement between the student, the institution and the industry partner. InduLink records the confidentiality settings and who was permitted to see what; it does not itself assign ownership.

Confidentiality in practice

  • Each project has a visibility setting: public, institution only, or private.
  • Each file has an access level: team, mentor, company or administrator.
  • A student must accept the project's confidentiality acknowledgement, with its version and timestamp stored, before confidential material opens.
  • Every confidential file access is written to the audit log.

What you agree to when you create an account

  • You will not enter personal data about real people in this prototype.
  • You will not upload code with the expectation that it runs. Uploads are stored, never executed.
  • You understand that recommendations are proposals. They are not offers, guarantees or assessments of a person's worth.
  • You understand that a synthetic dataset is in use, and that it cannot support claims about real outcomes.

No guarantee

InduLink is a decision-support tool. It does not guarantee a placement, a result, a grade or employability. Compatibility scores are not probabilities of selection or success.

Changes

Terms are versioned. When they change, the published version and date are shown here, and material changes are recorded in the audit log.
